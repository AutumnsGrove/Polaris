Beats on screen at once

Two knobs: how often a beat is born (the ping — 2s) and how long it takes to cross the box. Travel time = k × 2s puts k beats on screen at once, evenly spaced, and slows the drift as k grows. Because the box is 38 wide, two beats land about 20px apart and three about 14px.

Should be driven by real pongs, not a fixed ticker: ws.ts sends ping every 2s and the server answers pong; each pong appends one beat to the DOM, and that beat animates across for 4s and then removes itself. Two consequences worth keeping: the box can never show more beats than pongs actually came back, and if a pong is late the gap between beats widens — the trace reports the connection instead of decorating it. CSS-only fallback for the steady state: two <use> elements on the same 4s keyframes, second delayed -2s, exactly as in option 2 above.
