Heartbeat in a box

J worked because the trace had 150px: four beats, 37px each. A box that fits next to Comet gets 38–62px, so the same line becomes 5–6 beats of ~9px — a row of ticks, not a waveform. There are two honest ways around that, and both are below: keep it static and let a few fat beats read as a living icon, or keep it small and let one beat travel across the box so motion carries "alive" instead of density.

A

Sweep, 50 wide

50 × 18

One beat travels left to right every 2s across a hairline box. Nothing is dense, so nothing blurs — you see the box is being written to. Closest small-box equivalent of J.

Fit: word gets 77px, which is not enough for "reconnecting…" — so down copy has to shrink to "retrying…". Down = the beat stops and the line breaks; nothing animates, which is the message.

B

Sweep, 38 wide

38 × 18

Same travelling beat, tighter. At 38 the beat's whole pass takes ~1.7s of travel and the box reads more like a small screen than a strip — and the word gets real breathing room back.

C

Ticks, 38 wide

38 × 18

No motion: four fat beats on a faint baseline. Honest about what a small box can show — it reads as "four signals, alive" more than as a heartbeat. The calmest of the five.

D

Tile, 26 square

26 × 26

The exact footprint of an icon button, so it becomes a fourth icon that happens to be alive rather than a new kind of object in the row. Three beats, square, no word pressure at all.

Six static beats with real air between them — the most J-like still frame, and the widest thing that fits at all. It costs the word: only "connected" / "retrying" fit beside it.

My pick: B — sweep, 38 wide, hairline box, before Comet. It's the only small-box option where what you notice is motion rather than density, so it still reads at 1× on a phone; and because a travelling beat doesn't need width the way a static trace does, 38 is enough — which leaves "reconnecting…" intact. A (50 wide) is the same idea with more room but forces shorter down copy. D (the 26px tile) is the pick if you'd rather have a living icon than a trace — it's the tidiest object and the most obviously "already part of the app". Avoid E unless you're happy shortening the down copy to "retrying 2s". Implementation either way: a ping/pong frame on the socket (the beat fires on each pong), and swapping the dot's flex slot for this box.
