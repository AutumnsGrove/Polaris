How Home Solar Batteries Actually Pay Back

A deeper look at real payback timelines, incentive stacking, and where the math falls apart — this week's deep-dive topic, configured once and revisited weekly.

The headline payback number is doing a lot of work

Most marketing quotes a 6-8 year payback window, but that figure usually assumes full retail net metering — a policy that's being phased down in several states as utilities shift toward time-of-use export rates.

Where incentive stacking changes the math

Federal, state, and utility-level incentives can be combined, but eligibility windows rarely align — a battery installed a year after the panels can miss a bundled rebate entirely.
