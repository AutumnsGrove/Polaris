2 — Pulsar Daily config

The hardest case: nine toggleable blocks, four of which reveal an inline textarea (plus a "Help me write this" wizard button) when switched on, plus a free-form custom-blocks list with add/remove. Shown here as one settings-group per logical cluster, with the sub-field nested inside its own row using a dashed divider rather than breaking out into a separate box — keeps the toggle and its own field visually bound together as you scan down the list.
